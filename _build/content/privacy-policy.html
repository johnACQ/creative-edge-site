Privacy policy

Creative Edge Landscaping Ltd., Vernon, BC, Canada. This policy covers creativeedgelandscaping.ca. Last updated July 2026.

What we collect

If you fill in a form on this site, we collect what you type into it: your name, phone number, email address, the town the property is in, what you are looking to build and your rough timeframe. That is the whole list. There is no account to create and nothing else is required.

Why we collect it

So we can call you back about your project. That is the only reason. We use it to contact you, to prepare a design and a quote, and to keep a record of the job if you go ahead.

Who sees it

Creative Edge Landscaping Ltd. and the customer relationship system we use to manage enquiries. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else for their own marketing.

Contact permission

Submitting a form means you are happy for us to contact you by phone, text and email about your enquiry. Message and data rates may apply to texts. You can stop texts at any time by replying STOP, and you can ask us to stop contacting you altogether by emailing brad@creativeedgelandscaping.ca.

Cookies and measurement

This site may set cookies to understand how people found it, for example whether you arrived from a Google or Facebook advert. Those cookies measure advertising performance. They do not identify you personally to us beyond what you have already typed into a form, and you can block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept while they are active and afterwards as part of our business records. If you would like your details removed, email us and we will remove them, other than anything we are required to keep for tax or legal reasons.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email brad@creativeedgelandscaping.ca and we will deal with it.

Links to other sites

This site links out to places like our Instagram. Once you follow a link off this site you are covered by that company's privacy policy, not this one.
