Design

See the whole yard before anyone digs.

Every project starts as a drawing. Planting, hardscape, lighting and the amenities you actually want, laid out in 2D and 3D so you know exactly what you are getting and exactly what it costs before a machine turns up.

Design considers the things that decide whether a yard works here: drainage, sun exposure, slope, soil, and how you genuinely use the space in August versus February.

Design packages

Pick the scope, not a mystery number.

Seven packages depending on how much of the property you are doing. Every one includes two rounds of revisions, a kick-off call to understand what you are actually after, and check-in calls while it is being drawn.

Full property

The whole lot. Landscape, planting and hardscaping design across front, back and sides, plus home exterior direction (paint, siding, windows, roofing), lighting design, and hand-picked furniture and décor.

Full yard, 3D

Complete custom design for front, back and adjacent side yards, brought to life in detailed 3D. Planting, hardscape, amenities and lighting.

Full yard, 2D

The same coverage as the 3D package, delivered as detailed 2D plans. The cost-effective way to do the whole yard properly.

Backyard

Turn the back into a proper outdoor living space. Hardscape, pergolas, firepits, outdoor kitchens, water features, fencing, pools and hot tubs, plus lighting.

Front yard

Curb appeal done deliberately. Walkways, paved areas, stairs, planting, lighting and the features that make the front of the house read properly from the road.

Planting design

A plant-focused refresh. Custom plant palette and layout for your style, climate and goals across front, back and side yards, with 3D visualisation of the planting.

Small space

Compact patios, side yards and rooftops. Same treatment as a full yard, scaled to the space, with 3D visualisation of the final design.
