Common questions

Good to know before you book.

Is the design consult really free?

Yes. We come out, walk the property with you, talk through what's possible and give you a budget range. No charge and no obligation. If you decide to go ahead, the full 2D and 3D design package is the next step and we'll explain exactly what that involves before you commit to anything.

How fast do you actually get back to me?

Same working day. We're in the office Monday to Friday, 8 to 5, so an enquiry that lands Saturday night gets picked up Monday morning. Getting back to people is the thing we're known for and we're not going to be precious about it.

Can you give me a rough number before I commit to anything?

Yes, and that's the point of the visit. We cost from built-in kits, so we can put a genuine budget range in front of you in minutes rather than making you wait weeks. It's a budget, not a final quote. Site access, drainage, rock and material choices can move it, and we'll tell you which ones apply to your yard while we're standing in it.

Do you build the pool, or the yard around it?

Both, with our own crew. The pool, the deck, the hardscape, the planting and the lighting all get designed as one space and built by the same people. You're not project-managing three separate companies who blame each other when something goes wrong.

How long does a pool build take?

It depends on the pool, the site and how much of the yard is in scope. What we will do is give you the schedule before we start rather than after, so you know what your summer looks like. Access, rock and weather are the things that move it, and we'll tell you which ones apply to your property while we're standing in it.

What's covered by the warranty?

Our work carries a two year limited warranty. We'll walk you through exactly what it does and doesn't cover in writing before you sign anything, so there are no surprises later.

Which towns do you cover?

Vernon and the North Okanagan. Coldstream, Lavington, Armstrong, Enderby, Lumby, and up to Salmon Arm and Sicamous. Pick your town on the form and we'll confirm we can get to you.

What happens after I send the form?

We call you back the same working day to hear what you're planning and book a time to come out. No sales pitch on the phone, and nobody is going to turn up at your door unannounced.

Are you insured?

Yes, fully insured, and we'll send you a copy of our certificate of insurance on request before any work starts.

When should I start if I want to be swimming next summer?

Earlier than most people expect. Design, permits and scheduling all happen before anything gets dug, and we run a full book through the season. Starting the conversation in autumn or winter is what gets you in the water for the summer rather than at the end of it.

Tell us what you're picturing.

We'll come out, show you the design for the whole space, and give you a number. That's it.
